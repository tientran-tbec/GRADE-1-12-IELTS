Mẫu câu

  • This is Mary. – Đây là Mary. → Hi, Mary.
  • That's Mr Long. – Kia là thầy Long. → Hello, Mr Long.
  • Is this Bill? – Đây có phải Bill không? → Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
  • Is that Nam? → No, it isn't. It's Minh.
  • It's = It is   |   It isn't = It is not
