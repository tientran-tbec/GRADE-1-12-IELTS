2

Đọc hội thoại và chọn True hoặc False.

Nam: Hi, Lan. How are you?
Lan: I'm fine, thanks. And you?
Nam: I'm fine, too. Goodbye, Lan.
Lan: Bye, Nam.

1
Nam asks "How are you?"
2
Lan is not fine.
3
They say goodbye.
4
Lan says "Hello" at the end.
