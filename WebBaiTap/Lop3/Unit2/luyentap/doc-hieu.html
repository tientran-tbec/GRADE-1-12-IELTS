Bill: Hi. I'm Bill. What's your name?
Nam: Hello, Bill. My name's Nam.
Bill: How old are you?
Nam: I'm eight years old.

1
Bill asks about Nam's name.
2
Nam is nine years old.
3
Bill asks "How old are you?"
4
Nam's name is Bill.
