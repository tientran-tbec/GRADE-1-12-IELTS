Unit 16 – Weather: Read the passage, then choose the correct answer / True or False. (Đọc đoạn văn và làm bài.)

A Day Out with Friends

One weekend, my friends and I planned a day out in our neighborhood. We woke up early, and the first thing we did was look outside to check the weather. The sky was clear, and the sun was shining, so we knew it was going to be a sunny day.

Excitedly, we decided to start our adventure by going to the local supermarket. On the way there, we passed by a delightful bakery with the sweet aroma of fresh bread. Even though we were eager to reach the supermarket, we couldn't resist stopping by to buy some tasty pastries.

After filling our bags with snacks, we continued on our journey to the supermarket. Once there, we explored the aisles, picking out fruits, vegetables, and other goodies for a picnic later.

As we left the supermarket, we noticed the sky becoming a bit cloudy. Despite the change in the weather, we were determined to make the most of our day. We decided to visit the nearby water park to cool off.

At the water park, we splashed and played in the refreshing water. Suddenly, the wind picked up, making it a bit windy. We didn't mind, though – it added more fun to our water adventures.

Later in the day, we visited a small bookshop to find a story to read. As we left the bookshop, we felt a few raindrops. It was becoming rainy! We quickly ran to a cozy food stall to enjoy some warm noodles while the rain fell outside.

Despite the unexpected rain, our day out with friends was filled with laughter, delicious treats, and exciting adventures.

A Day in the Park

One sunny afternoon, my friends and I decided to go to the park to play. To get there, we had to walk from our house. Mom told us to go straight until we reached the big tree, then we needed to turn left.

As we walked, we saw a funny sign that said, "Ice Cream Shop to the left!" We giggled and thought about having ice cream later. Continuing on, we reached the big tree, and Mom's instructions were clear - it was time to turn left.

Now, we were on a new path surrounded by beautiful flowers. We walked and skipped, feeling excited about our adventure. Suddenly, we saw a big playground up ahead. Mom had told us that the park was just straight ahead after turning left.

At the playground, we played on the swings, the slide, and climbed on the jungle gym. After having so much fun, we decided to explore more. Mom had said that if we wanted to go to the pond, we should turn right at the end of the path.

Following Mom's advice, we turned right and found a lovely pond with ducks swimming. We fed the ducks and enjoyed the peaceful scene. When it was time to go home, Mom told us to turn round and go back the way we came.

Our day in the park was full of adventures, and we were happy to have found our way around with Mom's directions.

5

6

7
The pond was located after turning left at the end of the path.

A Day at the Market

One sunny morning, my family and I went to the market to buy some things. The market was a busy place with stalls selling fruits, vegetables, and clothes. As we walked in, we saw a colorful gift shop filled with toys and candies. It was right opposite the entrance.

Our first stop was the clothing section. I found a lovely pink skirt that I wanted to wear for a special occasion. The skirt was displayed on a rack between two other skirts of different colors. Mom bought it for me, and I was so happy!

Next, we went to the food section where there were fruits and vegetables. I saw a bunch of ripe bananas near the apples. Mom decided to buy both for a delicious fruit salad.

Dad needed to buy some rice, so we went to the rice stall. The rice stall was behind the fish and meat section. Dad chose a big bag of rice, and we also got some fresh fish for dinner.

At the end of our shopping trip, we found a small stall selling T-shirts. It was between the shoe shop and the hat shop. I liked a green T-shirt with a funny cartoon on it. Mom bought it for me as a surprise.

It was a fun day at the market, and we found everything we needed!

A Day at the Zoo

One sunny day, my family and I visited the zoo to see many animals. We saw a big, tall giraffe with a long neck. It walked merrily and looked at us with its big eyes. Next, we saw a lion in a cage. The lion made a loud roar, and we all giggled.

Nearby, there was a pond with a lazy crocodile inside. The crocodile liked to sit near the water and roar softly. We saw a playful hippo splashing in the water. It made us laugh as it swam quickly.

In the zoo, there was a special area where animals could dance. We watched a monkey and a parrot dancing together, moving beautifully to the music. It was so much fun!

Before leaving the zoo, we visited the bird section. Some birds liked to sing loudly, making the place lively. We imitated the birds and sang along merrily.
