Amazing Countries

The world is a big, colorful place with many countries to explore. Let's talk about some of them!

America is a land far, far away. It's known for its tall buildings, famous landmarks, and the Statue of Liberty. People in America speak English, just like us.

Australia is where cute kangaroos hop around. It's also known for the Great Barrier Reef, a colorful underwater world. In Australia, people speak English, too.

Britain is a country with a queen and a royal palace. The double-decker buses and red telephone booths make it unique. People in Britain also speak English.

Japan is a land of cherry blossoms and sushi. The bullet trains are super fast, and there are beautiful temples to explore. In Japan, people speak Japanese.

Malaysia is a tropical paradise with lush rainforests and delicious food. The Petronas Towers in Kuala Lumpur are really tall! People in Malaysia speak Malay.

Singapore is a tiny city-state with modern buildings and beautiful gardens. The Merlion, a mythical creature, is a famous symbol. People in Singapore speak English, Malay, Chinese, and Tamil.

Thailand is known for its golden temples and spicy food. The elephants in Thailand are friendly and playful. People in Thailand speak Thai.

And then there's our country, Viet Nam! We have beautiful landscapes, from mountains to beaches. The traditional ao dai is our special costume. In Viet Nam, we speak Vietnamese.

Every country is like a different page in a giant book, and each page tells a unique and wonderful story!

My Daily Routine

Every day, I have a routine that helps me stay happy and healthy. Let's explore a typical day in my life!

I get up at 7 o'clock in the morning. It's time to start a new day! I wash my face, brush my teeth, and get ready for the adventures ahead.

After getting ready, it's time to have breakfast. I enjoy a bowl of cereal with milk and a glass of orange juice. Yummy!

At 8 o'clock, I go to school. I learn new things, play with my friends, and have a lot of fun. School is like a second home, full of laughter and learning.

When the school day is over at 3 o'clock, I come home and have a little break. I might play with my toys or read a book.

At 6 o'clock in the evening, it's time for dinner. I eat vegetables, rice, and sometimes a piece of chicken. A healthy dinner keeps me strong and energetic.

After dinner, it's time to wind down. I go to bed at 8 o'clock. Before sleeping, I like to read a bedtime story or listen to a lullaby.

And that's a day in my life! I follow this routine every day to make sure I have a happy and balanced life.

Every day of the week is special, and it's a mix of learning, fun, and family time!

Fun Months and Yummy Treats

In Vietnam, there are twelve months in a year, and each month is special. Let's talk about some of them!

January is when it's a bit chilly, and we wear cozy jackets. It's the start of the year, and we celebrate new beginnings.

February is extra special because it's the month of love. People exchange cards and gifts to show how much they care.

March is when the flowers start to bloom, and the world becomes colorful again. It's a lovely time to enjoy nature.

April brings warmer days, and we often have picnics under the sun. The grass is green, and the sky is blue.

May is when it gets a bit hot, and we start looking forward to the summer break. It's a time for fun and outdoor activities.

One exciting day for each person is their birthday! On our birthdays, we have a party with friends and family. There's cake, balloons, and lots of laughter.

When we feel a little hungry, we might snack on some tasty treats. Chips are crunchy and salty, and they make a great snack while watching cartoons.

For a sweet treat, we have jam. It's made from fruits, and we spread it on bread or crackers. Yum!

When it's hot outside, we love to sip on refreshing drinks. Juice is fruity and cool, while lemonade is tangy and perfect for a sunny day.

Of course, we also need to drink plenty of water to stay healthy. It's like giving our bodies a big, refreshing hug.

Fun and Skills

I am a little kid, and there are so many things I can do! I can ride a bike, and it feels like flying on wheels. Zoom, zoom!

In the kitchen, I watch my mom cook delicious meals. Sometimes, she lets me help stir and mix. It's like being a chef in our own home.

After dinner, I love to play the piano. I press the keys, and it makes beautiful music. It's like having a mini concert in our living room.

Another instrument I enjoy is the guitar. I like to play the guitar with my dad. We strum the strings and make happy tunes together.

On sunny days, I grab my roller skates, and off I go! I love to roller skate around the neighborhood, feeling the wind on my face.

When we visit the countryside, I get to ride a horse. It's a big, gentle animal, and it's so much fun going for a ride.

In the summer, my friends and I go to the pool, and we all swim together. It's a splashy adventure, and we pretend to be little fish.

Sometimes, I use the word but to connect two ideas. For example, I want to play outside, but it's raining. So, I find something fun to do indoors.

There are so many things I can do, and every day is full of exciting adventures!
