Exercise 19. Read and decide whether these sentences are True (T) or False (F). (Đọc đoạn văn, True hay False?)

My name is Victoria. I am a pupil at Archimes primary school. My house is far from my school. It is about 12 km so I have to go to school early. I usually leave home at half past six. This is how to go to school every day. I go straight on the road, turn right at the Lotte centre, continue to go straight for about 5 minutes, then turn right at the crossroad and keep going straight for about 5 metres. And finally, I cycle along the trees from where I can see my school across the street.

She studies at Archimes primary school.
