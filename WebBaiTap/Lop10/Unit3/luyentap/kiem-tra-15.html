📋 Quy định làm bài
  • Thời gian: 15 phút. Còn 10 phút hệ thống nhắc; hết giờ có thêm 5 phút gia hạn rồi tự động nộp.
  • Bài làm ở chế độ toàn màn hình. Chuyển tab, mất focus, thoát toàn màn hình đều bị ghi nhận và báo cho giáo viên.
  • Không dùng chuột phải, sao chép/dán, phím tắt. Đáp án và giải thích chi tiết hiển thị sau khi nộp bài.
Mark the letter A, B, C, or D to indicate the correct answer to each of the following sentences.
1
She’s a big ______ of Black Pink whose girls come from Korea.
2
When he was in high school, he ______ at the school on special days.
3
From the semi-final ______, people can vote for their preferred singers online.
4
Vietnam Idol included three stages: short ______ to choose participants, semi-finals and finals.
5
The show is organised ______ the best singers and composers.
6
The judges ______ an important role in any competition because they are decision-makers.
7
In each show, ______ will dress up and perform live on TV.
8
The interesting thing about the show is that the audience can vote ______ their preferred performance.
9
They expected ______ their idol at the airport.
10
No parents will not let their teenage kids ______ such TV shows.
Make compound sentences using “and, or, but, so”.
11
At that time there were many movies and TV series. People have to find them on the Internet.
12

13

14

15
