E13: Write a paragraph (120 – 150 words) about one of your family routines. (tự luận – xem bài mẫu)
1
Write a paragraph (120 – 150 words) about one of your family routines. Use the following questions as cues:
  1. What is the routine?
  2. How often / When do you do it?
  3. What are the activities? What does each family member do?
  4. How do you feel about the routine?
